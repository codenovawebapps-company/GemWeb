Ratnapura (City of Gems)

රත්නපුර · The Ancient Corundum Capital

Situated in the Sabaragamuwa province beneath the sacred peak of Sri Pada (Adam's Peak), Ratnapura is the historical heart of Ceylon gem mining. Its ancient riverbanks harbor layered alluvial gravel beds (*Illama*) rich in deep blue sapphires, rubies, and chrysoberyl cat’s eyes.

Primary Mineral Wealth: Royal Blue Sapphire, Padparadscha, Cat's Eye, Spinel
Mining Technique: Eco-friendly Gam Pathal (Artisanal Timber Pit)
Illama Depth: 10 to 50 Feet Underground
Historic Discoveries: The Star of Adam (1,404 ct), Blue Giant of the Orient
THE ETHICAL PATH

From Illama to Heirloom.

Sri Lanka is the global benchmark for ethical gem extraction. We preserve nature through generations of artisanal tradition with zero heavy machinery.

01

The Artisanal Pit (ගම් පතල)

Shafts are hand-excavated using rubber-tree timber scaffolding (*Kikiliya*) and fern linings that stabilize the soil without toxic chemicals or open-pit scars.

02

The Basket Wash (ඉල්ලම් ගැරීම)

Gem-bearing gravel (*Illama*) is washed in conical bamboo wicker baskets (*Vatti*) inside natural rivers. The heavy corundum naturally settles at the vortex.

03

Master Lapidary (කැපීම හා ඔපදැමීම)

Sri Lankan master cutters use traditional hand-guided wheels (*Hanapane*) to orient optical crystal axes, maximizing natural color saturation and brilliance over yield.

04

NGJA & Global Certification

Every stone is sealed with government provenance clearance from the National Gem & Jewellery Authority of Sri Lanka and independent GIA/GRS gemological dossiers.
